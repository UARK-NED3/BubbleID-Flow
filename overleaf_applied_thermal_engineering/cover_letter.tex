\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please consider our manuscript, ``BubbleID-Flow: Reproducible Machine-Vision Quantification of Projected Vapor Coverage in Subcooled Flow Boiling,'' for publication as an Original Research Article in \textit{Applied Thermal Engineering}.

The manuscript develops an auditable workflow for converting high-speed FC-72 flow-boiling images into two-dimensional projected vapor coverage. A one-class Mask R-CNN model fine-tuned on 130 annotated images is compared with fixed and learned pixel baselines. The analysis exposes the same-sequence holdout limitation, replaces independent-frame uncertainty with a moving-block bootstrap, reconstructs electrical heat flux and mass flux from documented geometry, and tests whether projected coverage improves held-out prediction of the measured surface-temperature response. It does not in this dataset, which defines a conservative and reproducible claim boundary. The work is aligned with \textit{Applied Thermal Engineering} because it connects image-based measurement quality to thermal forcing, measured test-section temperatures, and validation requirements for flow-boiling diagnostics.

The corresponding author will confirm publication status, concurrent-submission status, author approval, funding, competing interests, and data-sharing language before this letter is used for submission.

\closing{Sincerely,}
